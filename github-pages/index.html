Jayeeta job pipeline

This is a personal tool used only by Jayeeta Das to organise her own job search. It is not offered to anyone else, and no other person can sign in to it.

Once a day it collects job adverts from public job boards, keeps the ones that match her field, and prepares a short list for her to review. With her permission it connects to her own Google account to:

  • read her Gmail for application confirmation emails, so the same job is not listed twice;
  • create email drafts in her Gmail, which she reviews and sends herself;
  • save the files it prepares to a folder it creates in her Google Drive.

See the privacy policy for exactly what data is used and where it is kept.
