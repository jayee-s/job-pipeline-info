Privacy policy: Jayeeta job pipeline

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Jayeeta job pipeline is a personal tool. Its only user is its owner, Jayeeta Das, and it only ever accesses her own Google account.

Google data it uses

  • Gmail (read): searches for application confirmation emails and reads their sender, subject, date and a short snippet, to record which companies have already been applied to.
  • Gmail (compose): creates email drafts. Drafts are not sent automatically; the owner reviews and sends them herself.
  • Google Drive (files it creates only): creates one folder and saves the job notes, cover letters and tracker it prepares. It cannot see any other file in Drive.

Where data is kept

Data read from Google is stored only in the owner's private GitHub repository and in her own Google Drive. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising. Email content is not sent to any AI service; job adverts and facts from the owner's CV are sent to an AI model to score how well a job fits.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account page under Security > Third-party apps & services. Stored data can be removed by deleting the private repository and the Drive folder.
